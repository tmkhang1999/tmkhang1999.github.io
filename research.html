My work sits at the intersection of computational colour, image physics, and deep learning. I am especially interested in problems where physical constraints can guide model training.

Cross-Illumination Albedo Invariance:
Lightness-Stable Intrinsic Decomposition from Real Multi-Illumination Photographs

A single photograph cannot tell a dark surface from a dimly lit one, so lighting leaks into the recovered albedo. CIAI trains on pairs of real photographs of one scene under different lighting and requires their albedos to agree. It makes the albedo markedly more stable in lightness; it does not fix colour, because the real pairs barely change light colour and the loss sees only brightness. The next stage separates shading luminance from shading chroma.

Minh Khang Tran, Seyed Ali Amirshahi, Luis Gomez Robledo, Sezer Karaoglu, Theo Gevers

HyRES: Residual-Enhanced Hybrid Image Compression

Keeps a quality-1 JPEG as the base layer and adds a 10M-parameter neural codec for what the JPEG got wrong, so every file is still a valid JPEG. On Kodak it reaches 37.41 dB at 1.56 bpp, 2.1 dB above JPEG at the same file size.

Minh Khang Tran, Dinh Hoang Dai  ·  Mentor: Damien Muselet

TextMAE: Text-Guided Masked Autoencoder for Image Compression

Sends only the informative patches of an image plus a one-sentence caption. A Masked Autoencoder and a caption-guided Stable Diffusion refiner rebuild the rest at very low bit rates.

3D Multi-View Stereo Reconstruction: ETH3D and Middlebury Chess

A full classical stereo pipeline (calibration, SGBM disparity, ICP fusion, meshing) on ETH3D and Middlebury Chess. The fused point cloud reached a Chamfer distance of 0.043 m and F1 0.638.

Multi-Spectral Vision System for Quality Inspection of In-Coupler Gratings in AR Displays

A 450–950 nm hyperspectral inspection pipeline for AR-waveguide gratings, built with Dispelix Oy. Three metrics separated all 5 defect types, and a 4-operator Gage R&R study kept measurement error under 12%.
