Selected Work

Cross-Illumination Albedo Invariance: lightness-stable intrinsic decomposition

Trains on real photographs of one scene under different lighting so the recovered albedo keeps its lightness when the light changes: 28–39% less lightness variation in matched ablations and 7–14% lower albedo error on unseen coloured-light renderings, with 18.5M trained parameters. The next stage extends it to colour.
